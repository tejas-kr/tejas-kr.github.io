🌿 Golden Rules to Live By
  • Eat within 30–45 minutes of waking up every day
  • Drink at least 3 litres of water daily — no excuses
  • Never skip your protein shake; it counts toward your goals
  • Cook chicken grilled or steamed with minimal oil always
  • Stick to the plan — results demand time, not shortcuts
  • Sleep 7–8 hours; recovery is where real gains are made
💪 Why This Plan Works
  • High protein preserves muscle while your body sheds fat
  • Balanced carbs keep your energy stable throughout the day
  • ~1580–1700 kcal creates a healthy, sustainable calorie deficit
  • Mid-morning whey shake curbs cravings before they strike
  • Light dinners support better digestion and deeper sleep
  • Sunday biryani = your well-earned reward for 6 days of discipline 🍛
